# Urban public spaces (UEFN)

Source: public-space-design skill, *Urban Design Skills* (MIT, A. Bhardwaj) — Gehl, PPS, Alexander, Sitte.

## Typologies

| Type | Real | uu | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small square | 15–21 m | 1536–2048 | best-loved size; café edges, one focal object |
| Civic plaza | 30–100 m | 3072–6000 (bigger only if strongly enclosed) | hard, formal, town hall/monument |
| Pocket park | 10–30 m | 1024–3072 | one lot; trees, benches, mural |
| Neighbourhood park | 70–250 m | compress to 4096–10240 | lawn, play, paths, pond |
| Linear park / greenway | 10–30 m wide | 1024–3072 wide | old rail line, canal side |
| Waterfront promenade | 8–20 m wide | 1024–2048 wide | hard edge, railings, views |
| Playground | 20–70 m | 2048–4096 | fenced, climbables |
| Community garden | 30–70 m | 3072–4096 | plots, sheds, fences — cover rich |

## Proportion & enclosure

- **Width = 1–2× surrounding building height** (Sitte): 3-storey edges (1152) → 1152–2304; 6-storey (2304) → 2304–4608.
- Enclose 3–4 sides; streets enter at corners.
- Past ~100 m real a space feels empty → split into rooms with tree rows (800–1500 uu tall), 1–3 step level changes, canopies 256–384 high.
- Monument off-centre; main desire line clear.
- Convex, enclosed shapes; leftover space reads unfinished.

## Gehl's 12 criteria as game checks

| # | Criterion | UEFN check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traffic protection | bollards/planters between road and plaza |
| 2 | Safety / overlook | ≥ 2 sides with windows/balconies; no unintended dark pockets |
| 3 | Shelter | arcades/canopies ≥ 10 % of area |
| 4 | Walk | paths on desire lines; 256–384 wide, promenades 512+ |
| 5 | Linger | lean-on edges: low walls, columns, railings (~100 uu) |
| 6 | Sit | benches, steps, planter edges (~45 uu); secondary ≥ primary |
| 7 | See | 5000–10000 uu sightline across main space; frame a landmark |
| 8 | Talk | niches/alcoves; NPC dialogue spots |
| 9 | Play | something interactive per sub-area |
| 10 | Human scale | eye-level detail 0–200 uu |
| 11 | Climate | sun and shade both available |
| 12 | Delight | water, art, planting, accent lights |
Score 1–5; average ≥ 4, none below 3.

## Placemaking → gameplay

- **Power of 10+:** ≥ 10 things to do in the main space, ≥ 3 per sub-area (shop, quest-giver, collectible, vantage, fountain, vehicle spawn, mini-game…).
- **Triangulation:** two attractors side by side make strangers meet → encounters.
- **Edge effect:** shops, doors, cover, loot along edges; centre = open stage.
- **Leave 30–40 % open** for combat, events, building.
- Reachable from every adjacent street; visible from a distance.

## City network

- Square/pocket park within ~1 block of everywhere; neighbourhood park within 2–3 blocks.
- Central plaza at the main junction, beside the civic/landmark building.
- Greenways and tree-lined streets link parks into a calmer second route network.

## Lighting

Paths 1× warm · gathering 2–3× warm · play 2× neutral · features 5×+ accent. Lamps 300–500 uu tall; no highway poles in plazas (`lighting`).

## Anti-patterns

Empty plaza · fortress park (fenced, one entrance) · windswept tower base · dead edge (blank walls/garages) · parking on the best spot · over-designed (no free floor) · sunless square · unreachable gem.
