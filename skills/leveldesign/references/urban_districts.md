# Urban districts & transect (UEFN)

Source: zoning-and-codes, mixed-use and urban-regeneration skills, *Urban Design Skills* (MIT, A. Bhardwaj).

## Transect T1–T6

| Zone | Character | Storeys (uu) | Front setback | Lot width | Coverage | Streets | Frontages |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 Natural** | wilderness, water | — | — | — | 0–5 % | trails | — |
| **T2 Rural** | farms, barns | 1–2 (384–768) | 1024–3072 | large | 10–20 % | rural road, no curb | fence, hedge |
| **T3 Suburban** | detached houses | 1–3 (≤ 1152) | 512–1024 | 2–3 cells | 30–50 % | curvy local | porch, fence |
| **T4 General urban** | rowhouses, walk-ups, corner shops | 2–5 (768–1920) | 256–512 | 1–2 cells | 50–70 % | neighbourhood/commercial | stoop, dooryard, porch, forecourt |
| **T5 Urban centre** | main street, mid-rise mixed use | 4–8 (1536–3072) | 0–256 build-to | 1–3 cells | 70–90 % | avenue, boulevard | shopfront, gallery, arcade |
| **T6 Urban core** | towers, civic monuments | 6–30+ | 0–128 | 3–6 cells | 80–100 % | boulevard, passage, alley | shopfront, arcade |

1. Centre(s) T5/T6 (main junction, station, waterfront).
2. Ring outward T4 → T3 → T2 → T1 to the map edge.
3. Change zone at natural seams (park, river, major street), never mid-block.
4. Never jump two zones.
5. Main/transit routes may run one zone hotter.
6. Step heights down toward parks, water, lower zones.

T5 ≥ 80 % and T6 ≥ 90 % of facade on the build-to line → continuous street wall.

## Land-use mix

| District | Residential | Commercial | Civic | Open space | Streets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suburb | 60–75 % | 5–10 % | 5–8 % | 10–15 % | 15–20 % |
| Neighbourhood | 45–60 % | 10–20 % | 8–12 % | 8–12 % | 20–25 % |
| Downtown | 30–45 % | 25–40 % | 8–12 % | 5–10 % | 22–28 % |
| Core | 20–35 % | 35–50 % | 5–10 % | 5–8 % | 25–30 % |

Shops/cafés on main streets and corners, homes above. Every neighbourhood: ≥ 1 corner shop, 1 playground/pocket park, 1 civic building. Arterials get commercial ground floors.

Believable facility counts (per real residents): café 500 · playground 500 · supermarket 3–5k · primary school 3–5k · clinic/community centre 5–10k · library, secondary school 10–20k · sports centre 15–25k. A ~5k-person game town: 2–4 cafés, supermarket, school, clinic, hall, 2+ playgrounds, a park.

## Special districts

- **Industrial:** footprints 4–10 cells, 1–3 tall storeys (512–768), yards, rail spurs, tanks, cranes, chain-link; lanes 640, radii 768+. Good map edge or arena.
- **Adaptive reuse:** warehouse lofts, market in a train shed, park on a rail line — old + new = varied building age.
- **Old town:** irregular streets, H:W 1:1–2:1, low-rise high coverage, pavers, no cars, landmark church/tower.
- **Ruins / post-apocalypse:** plan a normal city first, then collapse 20–40 % of buildings, rubble streets, overgrow T4/T5 — decay needs a believable plan underneath.
- **Waterfront:** promenade 1024–2048, active ground floors facing water, piers as landmarks.
- **Campus / base:** quads, one controlled gate, internal loop road.
- **Transit hub:** station = landmark + plaza; densest blocks within 1 block; taxi/limo ranks.

## District identity

Name · transect zone · one prop/material family · landmark visible from its entries · gateway at each entry · signature main street · lighting accent colour.
